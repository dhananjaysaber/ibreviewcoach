IB SEHS IA frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions students ask most about the SEHS IA.

What is the IB SEHS IA?

It is one individual scientific investigation. You write a research question, collect your own data, analyse it and write a report. For the course first assessed in 2026 it is called the scientific investigation.

How many marks is the SEHS IA worth?

It is marked out of 24, with four criteria worth 6 marks each: Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation.

How much of my SEHS grade is the IA?

The IB subject brief gives the IA as 24% of the final SEHS grade.

When is the new SEHS syllabus first assessed?

The new course was first taught in August 2024 and is first assessed in May 2026. The external assessment is Paper 1 (multiple choice and data-based questions, 36%) and Paper 2 (short-answer and extended-response, 40%). The IA is 24%.

What is the word limit?

The IB sets a maximum of 3,200 words for the report. Charts, diagrams, data tables, equations, calculations, citations, the bibliography and headers are not counted.

Are the SEHS IA criteria different from before?

Yes. The course first assessed in 2026 uses four criteria, each out of 6 marks, and half of the marks are for Conclusion and Evaluation. Older material that lists Personal engagement, Exploration, Analysis, Evaluation and Communication is for the previous course.

Can I get my SEHS IA checked before I submit it?

Yes. You can ask your teacher for feedback within your school's rules, and you can upload your draft to IB IA Review for criteria-based feedback, an annotated copy and a fix plan.

Does IB IA Review write my IA?

No. It coaches your writing by showing what to fix and why. You write your own work.

Is IB IA Review free?

Your first review is free and shows the Research design feedback. A full review covers all four criteria.

Who approves the reports?

Paid reviews are marked and approved by an authorised IB moderator before release.

Is IB IA Review official?

No. It is an independent coaching service and is not affiliated with the IB. Your teacher gives your official marks.

What makes a good SEHS research question?

It names one independent variable, one measurable dependent variable and a clear group, and it can be tested safely at school.

Is Personal engagement still marked in the SEHS IA?

No. In the course first assessed in 2026, Personal engagement and Communication are no longer a separate criterion. The four criteria are Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation.

Can I work with classmates on the SEHS IA?

Yes, in groups of up to three, if your school facilitates it. Within a group you use a different independent variable, or the same independent variable with a different dependent variable, or different data from a larger shared data set. You must not present the same raw data as another student, and all writing, including the method, must be your own.

Can I give participants caffeine, supplements or energy drinks?

No. IB examiner instructions for the sciences do not allow experiments that give participants substances such as alcohol, drugs, medicines, supplements, caffeine or energy drinks. They also do not allow experiments with body fluids.

Do I need consent to test participants?

Yes. Your teacher must approve the plan, you need written consent from each participant, and participants under 16 need written consent from a parent or guardian. If the activity is moderate to vigorous, you also need a written declaration of good physical health, for example a PAR-Q.

Can I use a database or simulation instead of collecting my own data?

The IB guide lists several approaches that can be used alone or together: hands-on laboratory work, fieldwork, spreadsheet analysis and modelling, extracting data from a database, and using a simulation. Whatever you choose, you must collect or select quantitative data and analyse it, and your report must explain how the data were gathered or selected. Agree your plan with your teacher.

What must be at the start of the report?

The title, your candidate personal code, the personal codes of any group members, and your word count. You do not need a cover page or contents page.

Will my teacher read my draft?

Teachers read and advise on one draft, giving oral or written advice on how to improve it, but they do not edit it. The next version you hand in is the final version.

How big can a group be?

Collaboration is optional, and groups can have up to three students. Each student must investigate their own research question and write their own report. A group report is not allowed.

Can I use statistics in my SEHS IA?

Yes, if the test fits your design and you explain what the result means. You do not have to use a test, but you do have to handle variation and uncertainty.
