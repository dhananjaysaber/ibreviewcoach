SEHS IA checklist before you submit

Use this checklist on your draft. If you cannot tick a box, that is the next thing to fix.

Research design

  • My research question names the independent variable, the dependent variable and the group.
  • I explain the science behind my expected result.
  • I describe my method with exact equipment, units, timings and instructions.
  • I list the variables I controlled and how I controlled them.
  • I explain my choice of participants and sample size.
  • I describe the safety and ethical steps I took, including teacher approval and written consent from each participant.
  • I did not give participants any substance and did not use body fluids.

Data analysis

  • My raw data are in clear tables with titles and units.
  • I processed the data in a way that suits my question.
  • I show one worked example of each calculation.
  • My graphs have labelled axes, units and a title that states the finding.
  • I show variation or uncertainty.
  • I explain any statistical test I used.

Conclusion

  • My first sentence answers the research question.
  • I support the answer with numbers from my analysis.
  • I compare my result with accepted science or published research.
  • I discuss the practical implications, based on my evidence.
  • I do not claim more than my data can show, and I stay within the people and conditions I tested.

Evaluation

  • I name specific strengths and limitations.
  • I explain how each limitation could have changed my data.
  • I say which limitations matter most.
  • I suggest a realistic improvement for each limitation that targets its cause.
  • I suggest what could be investigated next.

Final checks

  • My report is no longer than 3,200 words, not counting items my teacher confirms are excluded.
  • The key evidence is in the main report, not only in an appendix.
  • All sources are cited and my work is my own.
  • The report is clear and easy to follow, with figures and tables numbered.
  • I have a copy saved and I know my school deadline.
