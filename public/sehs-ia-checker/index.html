SEHS IA review and checker for IB Sports Science students

IB IA Review reads your SEHS IA draft against the four criteria and shows you what to fix before you hand it in. We coach your writing. We never write it for you.

What you get

  • Feedback against Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation.
  • An annotated copy of your IA, with comments placed where they apply.
  • A step-by-step fix plan: what to change, why it matters and how to check it is done.
  • A progress view that tracks the fixes you have completed.
  • Paid reviews are marked and approved by an authorised IB moderator before they are released to you.

Try it free

Your first review is free. It shows the Research design feedback so you can see how the review works before you decide on a full review.

How it works

  1. Upload your draft as a document.
  2. We read it against the criteria.
  3. You get your feedback, annotated copy and fix plan.
  4. Fix the gaps and upload your next draft to see your progress.

Who it is for

  • IB Sports, Exercise and Health Science students working on the scientific investigation.
  • Teachers and tutors who want a second view of a draft.

What it does not do

It does not write your IA, and it does not replace your teacher. Your teacher gives your official marks, and any marks in a review are indicative.
