SEHS IA group work and collaboration

Under the new SEHS course, collaboration is optional. If your school facilitates it, you can work in a group of up to three students. The report is always individual.

The rules

  • Groups can have no more than three students, and students may organise their own groups.
  • Your teacher guides the group so that everyone is fully engaged.
  • Each student investigates their own research question.
  • A report by the group is not allowed. All writing, including the description of the method, must be done by you.
  • You must not present the same set of raw data as another student.

How your investigation must differ

Within a group, each student does one of the following:

  • manipulates a different independent variable from the others
  • uses the same independent variable with a different dependent variable
  • uses different data from within a larger data set collected by the whole group

Example

Three students share a sprint-testing session. One compares warm-up types, one compares rest intervals and one compares footwear. They run the sessions together, but each analyses their own data and writes their own report.

Class databases

If your class or school collects data for a shared database, and you use it, your work is treated as a database investigation. Your method then focuses on how you filtered and sampled the data from the whole database.

Collaboration is not collusion

  • Agree the plan with your teacher before you start.
  • Do your own analysis, graphs, conclusion and evaluation.
  • Never copy a partner's analysis or wording.
  • Cite every source properly. Missing or improper referencing is academic malpractice.
