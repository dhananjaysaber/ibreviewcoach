SEHS IA group work and collaboration

Under the new SEHS course, students can support each other in small groups during the scientific investigation. The report itself is still individual.

What you can share

  • A similar method or protocol, where it is appropriate.
  • Help to plan, practise and run the testing, within your teacher's rules.

What must be your own

  • Your research question. The independent or dependent variable must differ between group members where a method is shared.
  • Your data. The data you analyse must be unique to you.
  • Your analysis, conclusion and evaluation.
  • Your written report, written entirely by you.

Example

Two students share a sprint-testing protocol. One compares warm-up types, and the other compares rest intervals. They run the sessions together, but each analyses their own data and writes their own report.

Stay honest

  • Agree the plan with your teacher before you start.
  • Say in your report if you used a shared method and who it was shared with.
  • Never copy your partner's analysis, graphs or wording.
