  • SEHS IA criteriaThe four IB SEHS IA criteria explained in simple words: what each 6-mark criterion looks for, what moves you up a band and what to put in your report.
  • Research questionHow to write a focused IB SEHS IA research question: variables, population, context, common weak examples and ways to fix them.
  • Topic ideas30 starting ideas for an IB SEHS IA investigation across exercise physiology, biomechanics, nutrition, psychology and fitness testing, with notes on feasibility.
  • Data analysis helpHow to handle data analysis in your IB SEHS IA: raw data tables, processing, graphs, standard deviation, t-tests, uncertainty and common mistakes.
  • Conclusion and evaluationHow to write the conclusion and evaluation in your IB SEHS IA: answer the research question, compare with science, name specific limitations and suggest realistic improvements.
  • Common mistakesThe most common mistakes in IB SEHS IA reports, by criterion: vague research questions, missing detail, weak graphs, overclaiming and generic evaluations.
  • ChecklistA practical checklist for your IB SEHS IA draft, organised by the four criteria: research design, data analysis, conclusion and evaluation.
  • Word count and structureHow to structure an IB SEHS IA report: suggested sections, how to spend your word count and what to include in each part.
  • Using examplesHow to use SEHS IA examples and exemplars properly: what to look for in each criterion, how to compare with your own draft and why you should never copy.

  • FAQAnswers to common questions about the IB SEHS IA: criteria, marks, word count, topics, examples, feedback and how IB IA Review works.
