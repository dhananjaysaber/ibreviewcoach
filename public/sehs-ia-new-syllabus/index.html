IB SEHS IA guide

The new IB SEHS syllabus and IA: what changed for 2026

The IB revised Sports, Exercise and Health Science. The new course was first taught in August 2024 and is first assessed in May 2026. If you are using older notes, check what has changed.

Course structure

  • Three themes: physiology, biomechanics and psychology, with a focus on connected, concept-based learning.
  • The old options have been removed, and some of that content has moved into other units.
  • Skills run through the course: experimental techniques, technology and mathematics, and the inquiry steps of exploring and designing, collecting and processing data, and concluding and evaluating.

Assessment at a glance

  • Paper 1 (36%): Paper 1A is multiple choice, and Paper 1B has data-based questions.
  • Paper 2 (40%): short-answer and extended-response questions.
  • Internal assessment (24%): the scientific investigation, about 10 hours, marked out of 24.

The external papers are 1 hour 30 minutes each at SL. At HL, Paper 1 is 1 hour 45 minutes and Paper 2 is 2 hours 30 minutes.

What changed in the IA

  • Criteria: four criteria of 6 marks each: Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion and Evaluation.
  • Personal engagement and Communication are no longer a separate criterion.
  • Conclusion and Evaluation are now two separate criteria and together carry 50% of the marks, so higher-order thinking matters more.
  • Practical implications: your conclusion should discuss what the findings mean in the real world.
  • Word limit: a maximum of 3,200 words for the report.
  • Collaboration: optional, in groups of up to three. Each student has their own research question, their own data and their own report.

What stayed the same

  • The IA is still one scientific investigation, based on your own research question.
  • It is still about 10 hours and worth 24% of the final grade.
  • It is still marked by your teacher and externally moderated by the IB.

Old criteria versus new

  • Old course: Personal engagement, Exploration, Analysis, Evaluation, Communication.
  • New course: Research design, Data analysis, Conclusion, Evaluation.

If a website, example or video talks about the old criteria, it is for the previous course.

What to do now

  1. Read the new criteria with your teacher.
  2. Choose a research question that fits the new criteria. See our research question guide.
  3. Plan for the conclusion and evaluation from the start, because they carry half of the marks.
  4. Check your draft against the new criteria. IB IA Review is built around them.
